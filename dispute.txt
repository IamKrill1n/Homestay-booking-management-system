1. The Admin Resolution

Once a booking is disputed, it enters a queue for your Platform Admins to review. The Admin acts as the judge:

Scenario A (Guest is right): The homestay was fake or uninhabitable. The Admin clicks "Force Refund," which overrides the cancellation policy, sends 100% of the money back to the guest, and updates the status to refunded.

Scenario B (Guest is wrong): The guest is complaining about something trivial (like the Wi-Fi being slightly slow) to get a free stay. The Admin clicks "Dismiss Dispute." The status changes back to confirmed, and the 24-hour payout clock resumes normally.

Scenario C (Partial fault): The Admin manually negotiates a 50/50 split, triggering a custom refund/payout.
