How Payments & Refunds Work

1. Fee Structure

For Guests: 0% fees. What they see on the listing is exactly what they pay. 

For Hosts: Deduct a flat 13% fee from the final payout.

2. Booking a Stay

When a guest finds the perfect place, they pay the full amount upfront to lock in the reservation.

This amoutn is kept by the platform until 24 hours after checkout before paying the hosts.

3. Cancellations & Refunds

If a guest needs to cancel, the amount they get back depends on how close it is to the check-in date and which policy the host chose for their property.

Here are the three options hosts can choose from:

Flexible: * Cancel at least 1 day before check-in: 100% Refund

Cancel less than 1 day before: No Refund

Moderate: * Cancel 5 or more days before: 100% Refund

Cancel 2 to 4 days before: 50% Refund

Cancel less than 2 days before: No Refund

Strict: * Cancel 7 or more days before: 50% Refund

Cancel less than 7 days before: No Refund

What happens to the rest of the money?

If a guest cancels late and only gets a partial refund (or no refund), the 87% remaining money goes to the host, 13% to the platform.

3. When Do Hosts Get Paid?

To make sure everything goes smoothly, hosts are paid 24 hours after the guest checks out. This 24-hour window gives the guest time to contact us if there is a massive emergency (like arriving to find the house flooded or double-booked). If everyone is happy and the 24 hours pass seamlessly, we automatically release the money straight to the host's bank account.
